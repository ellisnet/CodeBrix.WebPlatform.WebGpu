This file travels with the CodeBrix.WebPlatform.WebGpu.Graphics.ApacheLicenseForever
package (tools/webgpu-graphics-crates/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES-WebGpuGraphicsCrates.txt;
a build program finds it beside itself as
webgpu-graphics-crates/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES-WebGpuGraphicsCrates.txt and reads it
with GraphicsCrates.ReadNotices). It covers the package's crate bundle: the
archive webgpu-graphics-crates.zip, which the platform's frontend build writes
into the application's frontend beside the platform's own crates and the WebGpu
package's.

The archive holds ONE crate, codebrix-webgpu-graphics, the first-party crate of
the CodeBrix.WebPlatform.WebGpu repository. It is that repository's own code,
covered by the repository's LICENSE (Apache-2.0). It has no vendored
third-party crates of its own: the crates it builds on (wgpu, glam, bytemuck,
codebrix-webgpu and every crate their resolution names) are carried by the
CodeBrix.WebPlatform.WebGpu package, and their notices come with that package
(THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES-WebGpuCrates.txt, read with WebGpuCrates.ReadNotices).

The crate's WGSL shaders are compiled into the application's WebAssembly file.
Three tone-mapping functions in them follow published outside sources; the
same three curves are written once more in Rust, in the crate's ToneMapper::map
(the curves on the CPU). Its Radiance .hdr decoder follows the logic of a
fourth outside source. Each stays under its own license; none is relicensed.
They are listed below.

2. aces_narkowicz - Krzysztof Narkowicz's ACES approximation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Source:  Krzysztof Narkowicz, "ACES Filmic Tone Mapping Curve" (2016),
         https://knarkowicz.wordpress.com/2016/01/06/aces-filmic-tone-mapping-curve/
Used:    the curve's five constants, in the WGSL function aces_narkowicz.
License: the post offers the code as "free to use under public domain CC0 or

3. pbr_neutral - the Khronos PBR Neutral tone mapper
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Source:  pbrNeutral.glsl in the KhronosGroup/ToneMapping repository,
         https://github.com/KhronosGroup/ToneMapping (PBR_Neutral/pbrNeutral.glsl),
         the Khronos 3D Commerce reference for PBR Neutral tone mapping.
Used:    the operator's constants and steps, in the WGSL function pbr_neutral.
License: Apache-2.0 (the license of the KhronosGroup/ToneMapping repository,

4. RadianceHdrDecoder - the HDR loader of stb_image
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Source:  stb_image.h by Sean Barrett, https://github.com/nothings/stb

         the flat, old-style and new-style run-length encoded scanlines, and
         the shared-exponent RGBE to float conversion, in the crate's
         RadianceHdrDecoder (src/lighting/radiance_hdr_decoder.rs).

         Barrett), and ALTERNATIVE B, Public Domain (www.unlicense.org). It is
