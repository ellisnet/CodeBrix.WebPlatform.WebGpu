GraphicsDemo

Two WebGPU applications of one CodeBrix.WebPlatform site. Each one needs a browser with WebGPU.

  • Graphics The model viewer's complete scene - animals walking and dancing round a chest, a bird circling overhead - with image-based lighting, a sky and high-dynamic-range tone mapping, and more WebGPU scenes on buttons (among them a skinned character and a morph-target shape).
  • Animation The animation demo scenes: easing curves, colour ramps, gradients, text, fades, keyframe tracks, transforms, a camera, layout, paths drawn on, followed and morphed, and a sheet of shapes, strokes, gradients, blends and clips, on one clock; and an animation drawn on the page itself.

Third-party notices (what the applications compile in) and the sample's notices (the models and the HDR panorama the backend serves)
