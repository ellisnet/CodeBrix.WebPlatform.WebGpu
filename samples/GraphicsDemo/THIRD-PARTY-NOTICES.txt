================================================================================
THIRD-PARTY NOTICES: the 3D models, the HDR panorama and the fonts of the GraphicsDemo sample
================================================================================

What this file covers: the model files the sample carries in
backend/GraphicsDemo.Assets/Models/, which the GraphicsDemo.Assets project
embeds in its assembly and the backend serves, converted, as model data; and
the HDR panorama it carries in backend/GraphicsDemo.Assets/Environments/,
which the same project embeds and the backend serves unchanged (the browser
decodes it); and the fonts it carries in backend/GraphicsDemo.Assets/Fonts/,
which the same project embeds and the backend serves unchanged (the
"animation" application reads and shapes them in the browser). The site
serves this file at /SAMPLE-THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt. The
sample is never packed. The NuGet packages the sample uses (among them
CodeBrix.Graphics3D.Gltf2 and CodeBrix.Imaging in GraphicsDemo.Assets) bring
their own notices and are not listed here. The notices of what the frontend
applications compile in are written by the frontend build into
backend/GraphicsDemo.Api/wwwroot/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.

The sample copies only third-party model files whose source and licence are
recorded here, and each such pack's own licence file sits beside its models.
The files of the first two packs are the same files the CodeBrix.WebGpu
repository's model viewer sample carries (samples/WebGpuModelViewer/Assets
there), the Mini Forest archer the same file its Graphics tests carry, where
the same sources and licences are recorded. Every model and panorama pack
listed here is dedicated to the public domain under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): they may be used for
personal, educational and commercial purposes, and credit is appreciated but
not required.

The other resources of the "animation" application - the caption file
(Captions/), the SVG documents (SvgDocuments/) and the Lottie files (Lottie/)
of GraphicsDemo.Assets - were written or generated for this sample and hold
no third-party content; its picture is the Cube Pets pack's colormap.png
(below).
